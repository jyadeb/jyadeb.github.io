Liberate Systems — RFP Response Assistant

Python · Anthropic API · Voyage AI · ChromaDB · PyMuPDF · Streamlit

A retrieval-augmented generation pipeline that extracts requirements from engineering RFPs and drafts responses from a firm's own past bid documents. Parses a 60-page RFP and produces structured, confidence-scored draft sections in under a minute.

Built a multi-agent version using AI with additional features, then tested its extraction and evaluation output against a hand-built evaluation rubric. This measured roughly 26% output variance on identical inputs and 6 missing mandatory requirements, descoping it to a human-verified assist rather than continuing with unreliable output.
